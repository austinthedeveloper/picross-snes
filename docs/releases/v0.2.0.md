The first release of Picross '94, a Super Nintendo demake of the
[Picross web game](https://github.com/austinthedeveloper/picross). It's
the free-play half of that game. This is version 0.2.0 because it includes
both the free-play milestone (0.1) and the polish milestone (0.2).

- **523 puzzles** from 5×5 to 20×20 in 19 collections, all from the web
  game.
- **16 murals:** big pictures split into 90 tile puzzles. The picture fills
  in as you solve its tiles.
- **Relaxed or Hearts:** play freely, or have every fill checked, with three
  hearts. Three hints a puzzle (a cell, a row or a column), undo and redo,
  and a clock.
- **Paint strokes:** hold A or B and move to fill or mark a run of cells.
- **Big cells on small boards:** 5×5 and 10×10 puzzles draw at double size.
  A thumbnail in the corner shows the picture so far.
- **Records:** best times, solves by size, play time, and the web game's ten
  ranks.
- **Badges and achievements:** a bronze, silver or gold medal for every
  collection, and 21 achievements. The result screen announces whatever a
  solve earned.
- **Battery save:** every record, plus one unfinished board to CONTINUE.
  Two copies are kept, so a power cut while saving loses only that save.
- **Sound:** twelve effects, from pencil taps to a solve jingle. Turn them
  off in Options.

**Not in this version:** story mode, the daily puzzle, the editor.

**Heads up:** this has been tested with host tests and scripted emulator
runs, but not yet on real hardware. Please report anything odd on
[the issues](https://github.com/austinthedeveloper/picross-snes/issues).

**Files:** `picross-94-v0.2.0.sfc` is a 256 KiB FastROM LoROM with 8 KiB of
battery SRAM. Open it in Mesen 2 or bsnes, or put it on a flash cart. The
`.sym` file is for debuggers, and the `.sha256` file is its checksum.
